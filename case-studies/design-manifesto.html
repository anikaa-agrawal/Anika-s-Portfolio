02

Step 1: Fixing the Trust Deficit Before Spending a Rupee

Before launching any ads, I audited the client's Instagram profile (_design_manifesto). Previously, they had run ads, but people were hesitant to convert because the profile itself had major credibility gaps:

What was broken: A literal typo in the bio ("Offficial"), incorrect business category, an irrelevant personal YouTube channel link cluttering the bio, and zero proper story highlights.

What I fixed: I overhauled the entire profile structure. Cleaned up the bio copy, set the right category, organized professional highlights (Why choose us, Real results, Our approach, The problem), gave the aesthetics a coherent look, and synced WhatsApp with automated greeting and away messages to ensure a smooth, trustworthy handover when prospects clicked through.

03

Step 2: Campaign Structure & Execution

Instead of overcomplicating things with multiple confusing ad sets, I kept the setup lean and data-driven:

Lean Setup Strategy

We set up 1 campaign, 1 ad set, and 2 ad creatives. By the end of Day 3, I noticed that the first ad was underperforming and burning budget without traction, so I turned it off immediately. The second ad carried the entire remainder of the campaign, driving all 22 high-quality conversations smoothly.
