Step 1: Fixing the Trust Deficit Before Spending a Rupee

Before launching any ads, I audited the client's Instagram profile (_design_manifesto). Previously, they had run ads, but people were hesitant to convert because the profile itself had major credibility gaps[cite: 5, 6]:

What was broken: A literal typo in the bio ("Offficial"), incorrect business category, an irrelevant personal YouTube channel link cluttering the bio, and zero proper story highlights[cite: 6].

What I fixed: I overhauled the entire profile structure. Cleaned up the bio copy, set the right category, organized professional highlights (Why choose us, Real results, Our approach, The problem), gave the aesthetics a coherent look, and synced WhatsApp with automated greeting and away messages to ensure a smooth, trustworthy handover[cite: 5].

Step 2: Precision Cold Targeting Strategy

Instead of relying on broad, weak targeting that burns ad spend, I built a highly dialed-in cold audience specifically tailored for high-ticket service buyers in the NCR region[cite: 11, 12]:

Audience & Targeting Breakdown

Location & Radius: Targeted business hubs across Delhi (+25 km), Faridabad (+17 km), Gurugram (+17 km), and Noida (+17 km), while cleanly excluding Agra to prevent wasted budget[cite: 11, 12].

Demographics & Roles: Focused on decision-makers aged 25–55[cite: 11, 12], targeting interests in Entrepreneurship (business and finance), Small Business Owners, Facebook/Business Page admins, and exact job titles including Owner, Founder, and CEO & Founder[cite: 12].

Purchasing Power Filter: Layered a strict household income filter (**Top 10% to Top 11%–20% in India**)[cite: 12] to ensure every rupee spent targeted individuals with the financial capability to afford high-ticket packages (₹9,999 – ₹24,999).
